# Learning centre

Who this is for: everyone who uses the portal, and administrators who
follow their team's training. The Learning centre teaches the portal at
your own pace: short lessons, animated explainers, guided tours over the
real screens and quizzes. Your progress is saved for you, on any device.

The lessons are in English for now; menus, buttons and quiz controls are
also in Khmer. In Khmer, a note at the top of the Learning centre, of each
lesson and of each guided-tour step says that the lesson text is in English.

## 1. Opening the Learning centre

Click **Work › Learning centre** in the menu (Khmer: **មជ្ឈមណ្ឌលសិក្សា**).
Every signed-in member of a lending organisation can open it.

The page shows:

- **Your learning paths** — the paths for the roles you hold (see
  [02-roles-and-approvals.md](02-roles-and-approvals.md)). Each path is a
  list of lessons with a progress bar, for example "3 of 6 lessons
  complete". If you hold no lending role, all paths are listed.
- **Other learning paths** — the rest, closed. Click a path to open it.
- **Your badges** — one for each path you have completed.
- **Portal tour** — starts the orientation tour (section 4).
- **Team progress** — only if you may see the team report (section 6).

There are ten paths: Loan officer, Credit analyst, Credit approver, Loan
operations, Collections, Finance, Product team, Risk & compliance, Auditor
and Tenant administrator. Every path begins with **Finding your way
around** and **Roles, permissions and maker-checker**.

## 2. Taking a lesson

Click a lesson. A lesson has five parts:

1. **What you will learn** — the objectives.
2. **How it works** — a short animated explainer (for example a loan moving
   through its life, a disbursement's journal entries, a loan's days past
   due moving into collection stages, or scorecard points adding up to a
   grade). It plays by itself; use the arrows to step, the pause button to
   stop and **Replay** to watch again. If your device is set to reduce
   motion, it does not play by itself.
3. **Try it** — **Start the guided tour** opens the screen and walks you
   through it (section 3). The card names the demo account to practise with.
4. **Read more in the manual** — **Open the chapter** opens the user-guide
   section for the screen in a new tab.
5. **Check your understanding** — a quiz of three to five questions. Choose
   an answer for each and click **Check answers**. Each question then shows
   whether you were right and why. The pass mark is shown above the
   questions (for example "Pass mark: 3 of 4"). **Try again** clears your
   answers.

A lesson is **complete when you pass its quiz**. Opening a lesson marks it
**In progress**; finishing its tour shows **Tour done**. A later, lower
score never takes away a pass. **Next lesson** at the bottom takes you
through the path in order.

## 3. Guided tours

A tour dims the screen and highlights one part of it at a time, with a
short explanation, the step number and **Back**, **Next** and **End tour**.

- Some steps ask you to do something, for example "Click the highlighted
  item to continue". The tour moves on when you click it, and follows you
  into the dialog or page that opens. **Skip this step** moves on without
  doing it.
- Tours never click **Approve**, **Execute**, **Post** or similar buttons
  for you; they show you where they are. Anything you do during a tour is
  real, so practise in the demo organisation
  ([demo-walkthrough.md](demo-walkthrough.md)).
- If a step's part of the screen is not there — your role does not show
  it, or the list is empty — the tour skips that step.
- Keyboard: **→** or **Enter** for next, **←** for back, **Esc** to end.
- On a phone, the explanation appears at the top or bottom of the screen.

**Take the tour** appears next to **Help** on screens that have a tour
(for example Applications, Loans, Collections, Products, Scorecards,
Operations, Period close, Audit, Access & policy, Borrowers and Team
members). If a screen has several tours, it lists them.

## 4. The first-sign-in tour

The first time you sign in, a card at the bottom right offers **Start the
tour**, a two-minute tour of the menu, your work queues, search, the
status chips, the language switch, Help and the Learning centre. Click
**Not now** to close it; it does not come back on any device. You can
start the tour at any time with **Portal tour** on the Learning centre
page, or **Take the tour** on Home.

## 5. Badges and certificates

When every lesson in a path is complete, the path shows a badge and
**View certificate**. The certificate names you, the path and the date you
finished it. Click **Print** to print it or save it as a PDF from your
browser's print dialog.

## 6. The team progress report

Who can see it: people with the permission `learning.report.read` —
**Tenant administrator** and **Risk / compliance reviewer** by default.

Open **Learning centre › Team progress**. The table lists every active
member of your organisation:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Team member** | The person's email. Click it to see each lesson they started, their best quiz score and whether they finished the tour. |
| **Roles** | Their lending roles. |
| **Suggested paths** | Progress on the paths their roles suggest ("No lending role" if they hold none). Choose a path in **Learning path** to compare everyone on that path instead. |
| **Lessons completed** | Lessons whose quiz they passed. |
| **Last activity** | The date they last opened a lesson, finished a tour or took a quiz. |

Use **Find a person** to filter by email. The report only ever shows your
own organisation. Answers to individual questions are not kept; only the
best score is.

## 7. Questions

- **My progress disappeared after I switched device.** It should not:
  progress is kept by the portal, not the browser. Check you signed in to
  the same organisation.
- **A tour skipped most of its steps.** Your role probably cannot see that
  screen's buttons, or the list was empty. Try it in the demo organisation
  with the account the lesson names.
- **The platform operator sees no progress.** Progress is kept for staff of
  a lending organisation only.
