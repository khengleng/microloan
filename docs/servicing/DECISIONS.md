# Decisions and assumptions

Business rules chosen during the build, with who decided and why. Rules that
affect money owed are decided by the product owner, not assumed.

| ID | Date | Decision | Decided by | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | 2026-10-17 | When a borrower pays more than is due, the default is to hold the excess as an **advance** and apply it automatically to the next instalments on their due dates (before penalties and days-past-due are assessed). Prepayment (`PREPAY`) and refusal (`REJECT`) remain product choices. | Product owner | UC-26, UC-27. Existing contracts keep the handling they were booked with; the new default applies to new products. |
| D-002 | 2026-10-17 | **Non-accrual** is off unless a product sets a days-past-due threshold. When on, interest keeps accruing on the loan but is booked to interest in suspense instead of income, and moves to income when the loan cures. | Product owner | UC-62 |
| D-003 | 2026-10-17 | Loans are **composed from reusable, versioned policies**. Products offer a default and a menu per policy kind; each contract chooses within that menu and parameter bounds; resolved policies are frozen in the contract. | Product owner | See [POLICY_MODEL.md](POLICY_MODEL.md) |
| D-004 | 2026-10-17 | **Lock-in periods**: prepaying or settling before the lock-in ends carries a configurable charge (or is refused), set per policy and frozen per contract. Months are counted from the disbursement date to the value date of the repayment. | Product owner | UC-32 |
| D-005 | 2026-10-17 | Lock-in tiers replace the base prepayment and settlement fee while they apply; after the last tier the base fee applies. They never stack. | Assumed (sensible default) | Confirm with product owner |
| D-006 | 2026-10-17 | On entering **non-accrual**, interest already recognised but still unpaid is moved out of income into suspense (`operations.nonAccrual.reverseAccruedOnEntry`, default on). Interest collected while on non-accrual is recognised as income when received (cash basis); the rest moves to income on cure. On write-off or forgiveness, suspended interest is cleared against the loss rather than expensed. | Assumed (sensible default) | UC-62. Common supervisory practice; confirm with finance. Does not change what the borrower owes. |
| D-007 | 2026-10-17 | An **advance** can never exceed what the loan owes on the payment date (all receivables after this payment's allocation). A larger payment is refused and pointed at early settlement. While an advance is held the loan cannot be written off, settled for less, or refinanced until it is refunded. | Assumed (sensible default) | UC-26, UC-27. Keeps advances from stranding money on a closed loan. |
| D-008 | 2026-10-17 | Early payoff of flat-rate loans: `repayment.earlySettlement.rebateMethod` is ACCRUED (interest accrued by day count — unchanged behaviour), RULE_OF_78 or ACTUARIAL. Rule of 78 / actuarial apply to flat-rate products only. | Assumed (existing behaviour kept as default) | UC-33, TV-12. **Open for the product owner:** which method each flat product should use; some regulators prohibit Rule of 78. |
| D-009 | 2026-10-17 | Capitalized grace interest is COMPOUND by default (unchanged); products may set `pricing.capitalization.basis = SIMPLE` so no interest is charged on interest. | Assumed (existing behaviour kept as default) | TV-13, TV-16. **Open for the product owner:** whether student loans and holiday programmes should use SIMPLE. |
| D-010 | 2026-10-17 | **Impairment** of lending loans follows a tenant policy (REGULATORY rate per class, or ECL = PD × LGD × EAD by stage) proposed by finance and approved by risk (maker-checker). End of day posts only the change in each loan's allowance; write-off uses the allowance first; closure releases it. **No allowance is posted until a policy is approved.** Regulatory mode applies the class rate to principal; ECL uses the recognised receivables (excluding suspended interest) as EAD. | Assumed (no automatic expense without finance sign-off) | OPS-5. **Open for finance/risk:** approve the first policy (the NBC tiers are offered as the template), and the PD/LGD calibration for ECL. |
| D-011 | 2026-10-17 | **Recovery proceeds** (repossession sale, pawn auction, insurance claim) pay the costs of seizure, storage and sale first, then the loan in `recovery.proceedsOrder` (default interest → principal → fees → penalties). Costs paid from the proceeds are therefore borne by the borrower; costs the proceeds do not cover are expensed by the lender (5300) and are **not** added to what the borrower owes. | Assumed — needs the product owner | UC-65, UC-66, UC-68. Affects money owed: confirm the order per product and whether unrecovered costs may be charged to the borrower. |
| D-012 | 2026-10-17 | A **deficiency** after a recovery sale stays owed on the loan, which remains ACTIVE on its existing schedule: the amounts paid are placed on instalments oldest first, and interest keeps running under the contract. Writing the remainder off is a separate maker-checker decision (UC-69), after which recoveries post as usual (UC-70). Repossession needs `recovery.repossessionMinDpd` days past due (default 90). | Assumed — needs the product owner | UC-65. Affects money owed: confirm whether a deficiency should stop accruing (accelerated, fixed balance) instead. |
| D-013 | 2026-10-17 | A **surplus** is held as a payable to the borrower (2710) and paid out only when a second user approves. An approved **insurance claim** settles the loan at once against a receivable from the insurer (1410); the insurer's payment later clears the receivable. A claim filed without an amount covers the balance on the settlement day, interest included; any excess goes to the estate. | Assumed — needs the product owner | UC-65, UC-66, UC-68. Affects money owed to borrowers and estates. |
| D-014 | 2026-10-17 | A **financed insurance premium** is a product fee with `treatment: FINANCED` and `payee: INSURER`: it is added to the principal and credited to the insurer payable (2610), never to fee income, and remitted to the insurer under maker-checker. A pawned item is valued at weight × fineness (karat ÷ 24, or the purity given) × the price per gram of pure metal entered by staff; it may be forfeited, with a second user's approval, only after the redemption deadline plus `recovery.pawnForfeitureGraceDays` (default 0). | Assumed (sensible default) | UC-06, UC-66, LT-09. No live gold-price feed. |
| D-015 | 2026-10-17 | **Debt sale**: a loan at least `recovery.debtSaleMinDpd` days past due (default 90, never less than 1) or written off may be sold, approved by a second user within authority. Gain or loss = price − (receivables − allowance − interest in suspense), to 4400 / 5400; a written-off loan is carried at nothing, so its whole price is a gain on sale (not recovery income). The loan becomes SOLD and is no longer serviced. A sale is refused while an advance is held or a repossession or pawn sale is in progress; pledges are left as recorded (handing security to the buyer happens off-system). | Assumed — needs the product owner | UC-71. Affects money owed: once sold, the borrower owes the buyer, not the lender. |
| D-016 | 2026-10-17 | Recovery actions reuse existing permissions rather than new roles: write-off makers propose seizures, forfeitures and debt sales; write-off checkers approve them (claims and debt sales also within their approval authority); repayment posters record sales, custody, policies and insurer payments; reversal checkers approve money paid out (surplus refunds, premium remittances). | Assumed (sensible default) | OPS-8. Split into dedicated permissions if recovery is staffed separately. |
| D-017 | 2026-10-17 | **Auto-debit** (UC-16) runs at the start of end of day, before accrual, penalties and days-past-due, for every active mandate on a live loan in the batch's scope: on the instalment's due date, and again on retry dates. It asks for everything fallen due and unpaid less any advance held. Retry policy per tenant, default **3 attempts a day apart**; ACCOUNT_CLOSED / MANDATE_INVALID / NO_AUTHORITY are never retried. Each failure queues a `lending.auto_debit.failed` notice. The repayment is posted by the system principal `system:auto-debit` under the borrower's mandate; money collected that the loan then refuses goes to suspense. | Assumed (sensible default) | Only a mock bank exists (deterministic by account number); production refuses it, so no debit is attempted there until a bank integration is built. **Open for operations:** retry count and spacing; whether a failed debit should carry a fee. |
| D-018 | 2026-10-17 | **Channel and payroll files** (UC-18, UC-19) are CSV with a header row (formats in `docs/lending/OPERATIONS.md`). A file with any malformed line is refused whole. A file is applied once per content hash (line endings normalised; for payroll the employer, period, currency and value date are part of it), a line once per channel transaction id (payroll: employer:period:employee). A repeated id with different content is not applied and stays a reconciliation break. Lines not naming a live loan in the line's currency, and lines the loan refuses, go to suspense with the reason. Payroll lines without a loan reference are applied only when the national id belongs to a borrower with exactly one live loan in the file currency; the national id is kept only as a blind index and last three characters. Value dates may be at most 30 days back. | Assumed (sensible default) | The daily reconciliation is by value date and channel; QR and wallet receipts are held in suspense under channel MOBILE. |
| D-019 | 2026-10-17 | **Cross-currency payments** (UC-21) use the organisation's own published rates (the existing FX rate table, either direction, inverted when needed), or a mock feed in development. The bank buys the payment currency at **mid × (1 − spread)**, spread per tenant, **default 0 bp**, 0–1,000. The loan is credited the converted amount rounded half-up to the loan currency's minor units; the payment valued at mid less that amount is posted to **FX gain/loss (4400)**. Each currency's leg passes through the FX position account (1050) so both journals balance. A rate must be published on the value date (tolerance configurable, default 0 days), or the payment is refused. Reversing a converted repayment reverses the loan-currency leg; the foreign cash stays in the FX position for treasury to return. | Assumed (no spread charged until finance sets one) | **Open for finance:** the spread, and whether the spread is reported as FX gain or as fee income. |
| D-020 | 2026-10-17 | **Document retention** defaults (years, counted from the later of filing and the end of the customer relationship — no open application and no booked or active loan): KYC 5, application 5, loan 7, collateral 7, agreement 7, generated letters 7, other 5. A tenant may set a rule per category or per document type (type beats category). Deletion is refused before then and always under legal hold. | Assumed (sensible default) | DOC-7. **Open for compliance:** confirm the periods against the NBC record-keeping rules and AML law; whether the clock should start at the last loan's closure (as built) or at account closure. |
| D-021 | 2026-10-17 | Every uploaded document version needs **review by someone other than its uploader** (accept, or reject with a reason). Generated documents (key facts, quote and closure letters) need no review. Documents linked from older stores take their state from the borrower's KYC status (KYC images) or start pending review. For the checklist a document counts once uploaded and not rejected or expired; only acceptance is final. | Assumed (sensible default) | DOC-3, DOC-4. Submission is not blocked by a pending review, to keep today's origination flow. |
| D-022 | 2026-10-17 | **Expiry reminders** are raised once per document, 30 days before expiry by default (`DOCUMENT_EXPIRY_REMINDER_DAYS`), by a nightly job after end of day and on demand; an expired document is flagged and stops counting for checklists. A replacement version with a new expiry clears the flag. | Assumed (sensible default) | DOC-6. Notices go to the outbox (`document.expiry_reminder`, `document.expired`, `document.requested`, `document.rejected`); delivery depends on the notification adapter. |
| D-023 | 2026-10-17 | **Document access**: download links live 5 minutes by default (`DOCUMENT_LINK_TTL_SECONDS`, 30–900). A customer's documents are visible to staff whose grants cover the customer's branch; a customer recorded without a branch takes the branch of their latest application, and with none cannot be opened until a branch is assigned (no branch scope). Borrowers see only their own uploads and the letters sent to them. | Assumed (sensible default) | DOC-8, DOC-10. |
| D-024 | 2026-10-17 | **Invoice financing** (LT-18) runs on a revolving facility drawn only by advances against approved invoices, at the product's advance rate on the invoice face. The fee is the product's rate × invoice amount per fee period, a part period counting as a whole one and at least one period charged (TV-20); it is raised when money comes in for the invoice. Each collection clears the fee, then the advance; any surplus is the borrower's reserve, held as a liability (2310) and paid out under maker-checker. The drawn balance carries no interest (the template prices at 0%). | Assumed (sensible default) | UC-67, TV-20. **Money-affecting — confirm with the product owner:** whether the fee should accrue on the advance rather than the invoice face, and pro-rata vs whole periods. An invoice collection cannot yet be reversed through the API. |
| D-025 | 2026-10-17 | **Invoice recourse** (UC-67): if the debtor has not paid by the due date + `recourseGraceDays` (default 30), the end-of-day batch moves the invoice to RECOURSE. The fee stops at that date; the borrower owes the advance and fee at once, and the facility's days past due run from that date (so buckets, collection stages, non-accrual and impairment follow). No late penalty is charged on recourse arrears. A debtor may still pay afterwards; anything beyond what is owed goes to the borrower's reserve. | Assumed (sensible default) | **Money-affecting — confirm with the product owner:** grace length, whether the fee should keep running after recourse, and whether late penalties apply. |
| D-026 | 2026-10-17 | **Letters of credit** (LT-19): the fee is the product's rate per started fee period (default 0.5% per 90 days) between issue and expiry, never below the minimum fee, collected in full at issue. The commitment is recorded as an off-balance-sheet memo pair (9100 customer liability / 9200 commitments issued) that nets to zero, released as the bank pays or on expiry/cancellation. Issuing needs a credit approver within their authority. | Assumed (sensible default) | **Money-affecting — confirm with the product owner:** fee basis and timing (upfront vs per quarter), and refunds on early cancellation (none today). |
| D-027 | 2026-10-17 | **LC conversion** (UC-53): a payment under a credit books a term loan for the importer under the product named by the LC product's `conversionProductCode`, at that product's default rate unless one was agreed on the request, over the instalments agreed on the request; its upfront fees are waived so the beneficiary is paid the drawing in full. The loan has no application; the drawing is its origin. | Assumed (sensible default) | **Money-affecting — confirm with the product owner:** pricing of conversion loans and whether conversion fees apply. |
| D-028 | 2026-10-17 | **Syndication** (LT-24): the tenant is always the agent; shares are fractions to 12 places that sum to exactly 100% (or commitments that sum to the loan). Participants fund their commitments before disbursement. Principal, interest and penalties of every collection (repayment, advance applied, early settlement — and their reversals) are split by share, fees by the optional fee share; each non-agent part is rounded down to the currency increment and the agent takes the remainder. Tax is never shared. Losses (write-off, negotiated settlement) are not shared automatically: they are left for a credit decision. The loan receivable stays gross on the agent's books; participants' funding (2330), what is due to them (2320) and their share of income (5300) are separate accounts. | Assumed (sensible default) | **Money-affecting — confirm with the product owner / finance:** rounding to the agent, penalty sharing, the gross presentation, and loss sharing. |
| D-029 | 2026-10-17 | The `INVOICE_BACKED` template no longer requires the general `collateral.borrowing_base` capability: the invoice register with its advance rate is that product's borrowing base. Inventory-backed lending still needs it. | Assumed | Capability matrix regenerated. |
| D-030 | 2026-10-17 | **BNPL** (LT-11, UC-52): the merchant is paid the purchase price less the product's **merchant discount** at disbursement; the discount is recognised as income (4500) at once rather than spread over the plan. A **merchant refund** reduces the remaining instalments **latest first** (the next payments stay as agreed and the plan ends sooner; a pro-rata spread would leave odd partial instalments). Whatever exceeds the principal still owed was already paid by the customer and is paid back to them in cash with the refund. The merchant discount is reversed **in proportion to the refund** (the last refund reverses exactly what is left), and the merchant owes the lender the refund less that reversal, held as a receivable (1240) until collected; it is not netted against later settlements. Interest on the remaining instalments, if a BNPL product charges any, is left unchanged. | Assumed — needs the product owner | Affects money owed by customers and merchants. **Open:** whether the lender keeps its discount on returns, and whether clawbacks are netted against the merchant's next settlement. |
| D-031 | 2026-10-17 | **Credit cards** (LT-07, UC-39, UC-40): a card is the card side of a REVOLVING facility; its balance is split into purchases, cash advances and unbilled plan principal, which always add up to the loan account's principal. Purchases and cash advances credit cash on the day (no scheme settlement account). Interest is charged when a statement closes, not daily. **Grace period:** purchase interest is computed daily from each purchase and **waived for the cycle** when the previous statement's balance (purchases + cash advances + fees and interest owed at the statement date; plan principal not yet billed excluded) was paid by payments dated from the statement date to its due date; otherwise it stands in full, trailing days included. Cash advances bear a fee (rate × amount, at least the minimum) charged at once to 4520 and interest at the cash-advance rate from the advance date, never waived; outstanding cash advances are capped at a share of the limit (default 30%). The **minimum payment** is all charges owed + the billed plan instalment + the greater of a share of the revolving principal (default 5%) and a floor (default 10), capped at the statement balance; it is due `paymentDueDays` (default 21) after the statement, and the next statement cannot close before that date. **Payments** clear penalties, fees, interest, then cash advances, purchases and, last, unbilled plan principal (a plan prepayment); overpaying is refused. | Assumed (sensible default) | Common card practice; confirm the rates, fee, floor and allocation order per product. |
| D-032 | 2026-10-17 | **Leases / Ijarah** (LT-15): the lender buys the asset (the payout must go to its supplier, dealer or seller — never the lessee) and owns it until the end. Lessor accounting is the **net-investment** (finance-lease) method on lease accounts: the asset at cost less the capital share of rentals received (1250), rentals receivable (1255), rental income (4510) instead of interest; late charges on an Ijarah go to a **charity payable** (2800), not income. There is no depreciation and no operating-lease model. The **residual** is the negotiated balloon and is the purchase-option price. At the end the lessee either pays it (title then transfers — a record, nothing is posted) or, once every rental and charge is paid and the final instalment is due, **returns the asset**, which settles the residual in kind: the asset moves to returned assets held for sale (1260) at the residual value with no gain or loss; its remarketing is off-system. | D-033 | 2026-10-17 | **Card arrears**: a statement's minimum payment is met by payments made from its statement date until the next statement; a payment first cures older shortfalls, then the current minimum, and anything beyond reduces the balance but does **not** pay future minimums in advance.
