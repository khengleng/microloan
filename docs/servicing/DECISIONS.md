# Decisions and assumptions

Business rules chosen during the build, with who decided and why. Rules that
affect money owed are decided by the product owner, not assumed.

| ID | Date | Decision | Decided by | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | 2026-10-17 | When a borrower pays more than is due, the default is to hold the excess as an **advance** and apply it automatically to the next instalments on their due dates (before penalties and days-past-due are assessed). Prepayment (`PREPAY`) and refusal (`REJECT`) remain product choices. | Product owner | UC-26, UC-27. Existing contracts keep the handling they were booked with; the new default applies to new products. |
| D-002 | 2026-10-17 | **Non-accrual** is off unless a product sets a days-past-due threshold. When on, interest keeps accruing on the loan but is booked to interest in suspense instead of income, and moves to income when the loan cures. | Product owner | UC-62 |
| D-003 | 2026-10-17 | Loans are **composed from reusable, versioned policies**. Products offer a default and a menu per policy kind; each contract chooses within that menu and parameter bounds; resolved policies are frozen in the contract. | Product owner | See [POLICY_MODEL.md](POLICY_MODEL.md) |
| D-004 | 2026-10-17 | **Lock-in periods**: prepaying or settling before the lock-in ends carries a configurable charge (or is refused), set per policy and frozen per contract. Months are counted from the disbursement date to the value date of the repayment. | Product owner | UC-32 |
| D-005 | 2026-10-17 | Lock-in tiers replace the base prepayment and settlement fee while they apply; after the last tier the base fee applies. They never stack. | Assumed (sensible default) | Confirm with product owner |
| D-006 | 2026-10-17 | On entering **non-accrual**, interest already recognised but still unpaid is moved out of income into suspense (`operations.nonAccrual.reverseAccruedOnEntry`, default on). Interest collected while on non-accrual is recognised as income when received (cash basis); the rest moves to income on cure. On write-off or forgiveness, suspended interest is cleared against the loss rather than expensed. | Assumed (sensible default) | UC-62. Common supervisory practice; confirm with finance. Does not change what the borrower owes. |
| D-007 | 2026-10-17 | An **advance** can never exceed what the loan owes on the payment date (all receivables after this payment's allocation). A larger payment is refused and pointed at early settlement. While an advance is held the loan cannot be written off, settled for less, or refinanced until it is refunded. | Assumed (sensible default) | UC-26, UC-27. Keeps advances from stranding money on a closed loan. |
| D-008 | 2026-10-17 | Early payoff of flat-rate loans: `repayment.earlySettlement.rebateMethod` is ACCRUED (interest accrued by day count — unchanged behaviour), RULE_OF_78 or ACTUARIAL. Rule of 78 / actuarial apply to flat-rate products only. | Assumed (existing behaviour kept as default) | UC-33, TV-12. **Open for the product owner:** which method each flat product should use; some regulators prohibit Rule of 78. |
| D-009 | 2026-10-17 | Capitalized grace interest is COMPOUND by default (unchanged); products may set `pricing.capitalization.basis = SIMPLE` so no interest is charged on interest. | Assumed (existing behaviour kept as default) | TV-13, TV-16. **Open for the product owner:** whether student loans and holiday programmes should use SIMPLE. |
| D-010 | 2026-10-17 | **Impairment** of lending loans follows a tenant policy (REGULATORY rate per class, or ECL = PD × LGD × EAD by stage) proposed by finance and approved by risk (maker-checker). End of day posts only the change in each loan's allowance; write-off uses the allowance first; closure releases it. **No allowance is posted until a policy is approved.** Regulatory mode applies the class rate to principal; ECL uses the recognised receivables (excluding suspended interest) as EAD. | Assumed (no automatic expense without finance sign-off) | OPS-5. **Open for finance/risk:** approve the first policy (the NBC tiers are offered as the template), and the PD/LGD calibration for ECL. |
| D-011 | 2026-10-17 | **Recovery proceeds** (repossession sale, pawn auction, insurance claim) pay the costs of seizure, storage and sale first, then the loan in `recovery.proceedsOrder` (default interest → principal → fees → penalties). Costs paid from the proceeds are therefore borne by the borrower; costs the proceeds do not cover are expensed by the lender (5300) and are **not** added to what the borrower owes. | Assumed — needs the product owner | UC-65, UC-66, UC-68. Affects money owed: confirm the order per product and whether unrecovered costs may be charged to the borrower. |
| D-012 | 2026-10-17 | A **deficiency** after a recovery sale stays owed on the loan, which remains ACTIVE on its existing schedule: the amounts paid are placed on instalments oldest first, and interest keeps running under the contract. Writing the remainder off is a separate maker-checker decision (UC-69), after which recoveries post as usual (UC-70). Repossession needs `recovery.repossessionMinDpd` days past due (default 90). | Assumed — needs the product owner | UC-65. Affects money owed: confirm whether a deficiency should stop accruing (accelerated, fixed balance) instead. |
| D-013 | 2026-10-17 | A **surplus** is held as a payable to the borrower (2700) and paid out only when a second user approves. An approved **insurance claim** settles the loan at once against a receivable from the insurer (1410); the insurer's payment later clears the receivable. A claim filed without an amount covers the balance on the settlement day, interest included; any excess goes to the estate. | Assumed — needs the product owner | UC-65, UC-66, UC-68. Affects money owed to borrowers and estates. |
| D-014 | 2026-10-17 | A **financed insurance premium** is a product fee with `treatment: FINANCED` and `payee: INSURER`: it is added to the principal and credited to the insurer payable (2600), never to fee income, and remitted to the insurer under maker-checker. A pawned item is valued at weight × fineness (karat ÷ 24, or the purity given) × the price per gram of pure metal entered by staff; it may be forfeited, with a second user's approval, only after the redemption deadline plus `recovery.pawnForfeitureGraceDays` (default 0). | Assumed (sensible default) | UC-06, UC-66, LT-09. No live gold-price feed. |
| D-015 | 2026-10-17 | **Debt sale**: a loan at least `recovery.debtSaleMinDpd` days past due (default 90, never less than 1) or written off may be sold, approved by a second user within authority. Gain or loss = price − (receivables − allowance − interest in suspense), to 4400 / 5400; a written-off loan is carried at nothing, so its whole price is a gain on sale (not recovery income). The loan becomes SOLD and is no longer serviced. A sale is refused while an advance is held or a repossession or pawn sale is in progress; pledges are left as recorded (handing security to the buyer happens off-system). | Assumed — needs the product owner | UC-71. Affects money owed: once sold, the borrower owes the buyer, not the lender. |
| D-016 | 2026-10-17 | Recovery actions reuse existing permissions rather than new roles: write-off makers propose seizures, forfeitures and debt sales; write-off checkers approve them (claims and debt sales also within their approval authority); repayment posters record sales, custody, policies and insurer payments; reversal checkers approve money paid out (surplus refunds, premium remittances). | Assumed (sensible default) | OPS-8. Split into dedicated permissions if recovery is staffed separately. |
